## 5. THE 5-LAYER "ZERO-TOUCH" FAIL-SAFE TRIGGER SYSTEM

What happens if the victim collapses suddenly or loses consciousness before tapping the screen?

1. **Pocket Blind-Trigger (Physical Hardware Combo)**: Pressing Power Button 3 times rapidly triggers SSS inside the pocket without looking or unlocking.
2. **Fall & Immobility Detection**: Accelerometer detects high-G impact followed by 10s of zero motion $\rightarrow$ Auto-fires SOS.
3. **Voice Hotword ("SSS Help!")**: Spoken command triggers the beacon across the room.
4. **Lock-Screen Quick Tile**: 1-tap thumb button accessible without fingerprint or PIN.
5. **$2 NFC Silicone Tap-Band**: Waterproof wristband that triggers SSS when tapped against the back of the phone.
